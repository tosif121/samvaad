Samwad — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What this app is

Samwad is a call-center agent app provided to authorized agents. It handles inbound and outbound customer calls, lead dialing, call dispositions, callbacks, and related telephony work.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your agent username and login credentials.
  • Call information: phone numbers, call direction, timestamps, durations, dispositions, follow-ups, and callback details.
  • Device information: device model, OS version, and push-notification token used to deliver incoming-call alerts.
  • Contact and lead data synced from your organization's account.

3. Permissions we request

  • Microphone — required for voice calls.
  • Notifications — required for incoming-call and callback alerts.
  • Display over other apps — used to show the incoming-call screen.

You can manage permissions in your device settings; declining them may limit calling features.

4. How we use information

Your information is used to authenticate you, connect and manage calls, show leads, callbacks, and call history, and to keep the app working reliably (including connection status and diagnostics).

5. Sharing

Call and account data is shared only with your organization's backend systems that operate this service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data retention

Call records and account data are retained as required for business and legal purposes. Logging out removes the session from the device; contact your administrator to request deletion of server-side records.

7. Security

Sessions use authenticated connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting your administrator or us at the address below.
